One emoji for my dream vacation: **🛶 (canoe)** — a dawn paddle on a faraway mountain lake, gliding over still water as the mist lifts. Not a generic beach, not a postcard sunset: one specific dream, a quiet morning on a lake you have to travel to reach, where doing nothing in particular is the whole point of the holiday. Three candidates were weighed (beach umbrella, sunrise over sea, canoe) — the canoe won because it is the most specific and the most surprising: everyone else submits sand and sun, while one small boat on open water is a whole postcard scene in a single glyph.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes exactly one emoji `🛶` to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline — inside the competition gate).
